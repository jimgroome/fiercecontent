United Kingdom

6 content hooks

2027

Employment Rights Act rollout

Retail impact
Unfair dismissal protections change in January. Further measures include mandatory equality and menopause action plans for qualifying employers in April and flexible working reforms in October. Guaranteed-hours measures have no confirmed start date.
Technology relevance
Workforce management, HRIS, rota planning, payroll and employee records.
Content opportunity
What the 2027 employment changes mean for multi-site retailers managing large hourly-paid workforces.
Source · gov.uk

January 2027

Consumer subscription contracts

Retail impact
New consumer subscription protections affect pre-contract information, renewal reminders, cancellation and cooling-off processes for covered contracts.
Technology relevance
Subscription commerce, ecommerce checkout, recurring billing, CRM and customer communications.
Content opportunity
How retailers can redesign subscription sign-up, renewal and cancellation journeys without creating unnecessary friction.
Source · gov.uk

October 2027

Online marketplace VAT

Retail impact
The government plans to extend VAT collection responsibilities to online marketplaces for specified UK-business sales from October 2027. Detailed implementation legislation remains to follow.
Technology relevance
Marketplace platforms, seller onboarding, tax engines, settlement and finance.
Content opportunity
How marketplaces can prepare seller data, tax calculations and settlement processes for the new liability model.
Source · gov.uk

2027 reporting year

Packaging EPR and recyclability ratings

Retail impact
RAM 2027 applies to packaging placed on the market during 2027. Recyclability influences modulated producer fees for obligated businesses.
Technology relevance
Product information, packaging data, supplier systems and finance analytics.
Content opportunity
How retailers can improve packaging data and assess the commercial implications of material choices.
Source · gov.uk

1 October 2027

Deposit Return Scheme

Retail impact
Covered drinks containers enter the deposit system in England, Scotland and Northern Ireland. Participating retailers will need return and refund processes. Wales is following a separate policy path.
Technology relevance
POS, reverse vending, payments, store operations and stock reconciliation.
Content opportunity
How retailers can handle deposits and returns without slowing checkout or adding avoidable store workload.
Source · gov.uk

January 2027

Tobacco age-of-sale rules

Retail impact
From 1 January, sales of tobacco and related covered products to anyone born on or after 1 January 2009 are prohibited.
Technology relevance
POS, age verification, store operations and staff training.
Content opportunity
How convenience retailers can implement a birth-date-based sales rule consistently across stores and checkouts.
Source · gov.uk

European Union

3 content hooks

January–July 2027

Instant euro payments expansion

Retail impact
Payment providers in non-euro EU countries face phased obligations to receive and send instant euro transfers and verify payees. The obligations fall primarily on payment service providers.
Technology relevance
Payment acceptance, reconciliation, treasury and fraud prevention.
Content opportunity
What faster account-to-account payments could mean for retailers operating across European markets.
Source · ecb.europa.eu

2 March 2027

Unsold consumer goods disclosure format

Retail impact
The EU implementing regulation standardises disclosures about discarded unsold consumer products for businesses subject to the underlying reporting obligation.
Technology relevance
Inventory analytics, returns, WMS and reporting.
Content opportunity
How better returns and inventory data can help retailers reduce unsold stock and account for its disposal.
Source · eur-lex.europa.eu

18 February 2027

Battery digital passports

Retail impact
Qualifying electric-vehicle, light-means-of-transport and industrial batteries placed on the EU market require digital passports.
Technology relevance
Product data, PIM, traceability and supplier information.
Content opportunity
What retailers of e-bikes and other battery-powered products need from suppliers to support product information requirements.
Source · single-market-economy.ec.europa.eu

United States

3 content hooks

1 January / 1 May 2027

New state consumer privacy laws

Retail impact
Louisiana and Oklahoma introduce comprehensive consumer privacy laws in January. Alabama follows in May. Applicability thresholds and obligations differ by state.
Technology relevance
Customer data platforms, consent management, loyalty and marketing automation.
Content opportunity
How retailers can manage customer-data rights across an increasingly fragmented US regulatory environment.
Source · venable.com

1 July 2027

Connecticut surveillance pricing

Retail impact
Connecticut restricts surveillance pricing by retailers and third-party delivery services, with related online disclosure requirements for other covered businesses.
Technology relevance
Retail pricing engines, personalisation, ecommerce and governance.
Content opportunity
How retail pricing teams can use customer data responsibly as states place limits on surveillance pricing.
Source · cga.ct.gov

1 August 2027

New Jersey personalised grocery pricing

Retail impact
New Jersey’s Fair Price Protection Act restricts using shoppers’ personal information to charge different prices for identical grocery products. Most provisions take effect on 1 August 2027.
Technology relevance
Grocery pricing, loyalty platforms and customer analytics.
Content opportunity
How grocery retailers can distinguish legitimate promotions from personal-data-driven price discrimination.
Source · nj.gov

Keep an eye on

2027
watchlist.

Track proposals, planned milestones and changes already in effect.

  • EU Digital Fairness Act

    Follow legislative progress on consumer protection proposals covering online sales practices, subscription journeys and potentially manipulative design.

    Follow · commission.europa.eu

  • EU environmental claims and product information

    Consumer information and green-claims rules began applying in September 2026. In 2027, retailers can refine product listings, evidence and marketing approvals.

    Follow · eur-lex.europa.eu

  • UK marketplace product safety reform

    Monitor government proposals on online marketplace responsibilities and unsafe product listings. These are still proposals.

    Follow · gov.uk

  • EU right to repair

    Consumer repair rights began applying in 2026. The EU repair platform is now expected in January 2028, creating an opening to examine repair and after-sales models.

    Follow · commission.europa.eu

  • EU textiles digital product passports

    The EU plans to adopt product-specific ecodesign rules for textiles in late 2027. The requirement for retail products to carry passports will follow a separate timeline.

    Follow · single-market-economy.ec.europa.eu
