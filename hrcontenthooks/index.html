United Kingdom

8 content hooks

1 January 2027

Unfair dismissal reforms

Employer impact
The qualifying period for ordinary unfair dismissal falls to six months and the cap on compensatory awards is removed. Employers will need robust probation, performance and dismissal records.
Service relevance
HR consultancy and outsourced HR advice on probation reviews, manager training, performance documentation and dismissal procedures.
Content opportunity
How should employers rethink probation and performance management before the new dismissal rules?
Source · gov.uk

April 2027

Scheduled

Collective redundancy: organisation-wide threshold

Employer impact
The government plans an organisation-wide threshold for collective consultation, affecting employers managing redundancies across multiple sites. The detailed threshold and commencement requirements should be checked as regulations are finalised.
Service relevance
Workforce restructuring advice, consultation support, manager training and employee communications for multi-site employers.
Content opportunity
Are multi-site employers ready for the new collective redundancy consultation rules?
Source · gov.uk

October 2027

Scheduled

Flexible working reforms

Employer impact
Employers refusing a statutory flexible working request will need to explain why the refusal is reasonable, alongside identifying a permitted business reason. Consistent decisions and documented reasoning will be important.
Service relevance
HR policy reviews, flexible-working advisory services, line-manager training and employee experience programmes.
Content opportunity
What will the new flexible working rules mean for managers handling employee requests?
Source · gov.uk

Gender equality and menopause action plans

Employer impact
Mandatory action plans are scheduled for public authorities in March and private and voluntary-sector employers in April, following voluntary introduction in 2026. Scope and final requirements need checking.
Service relevance
Gender equality consulting, menopause support, benefits reviews, employee listening and leadership training.
Content opportunity
What should employers include in their gender equality and menopause action plans?

Scheduled

Fire-and-rehire protections

Employer impact
Stronger restrictions on dismissing and re-engaging staff on changed contractual terms will affect employers reviewing pay, contracts and working arrangements.

Enhanced dismissal protections for pregnant employees and new mothers are scheduled to take effect, increasing the importance of consistent leave, return-to-work and redundancy records.

3 content hooks

7 June 2027

First EU pay transparency reports

Employer impact
Employers with 250 or more workers must report annually and employers with 150–249 workers every three years, with the first reports due by 7 June 2027 under the directive. National implementing laws govern local obligations.
Service relevance
Pay and reward consulting, recruitment advisory, job evaluation, compensation benchmarking and HR analytics services.
Content opportunity
Are employers ready to explain their pay differences under the EU transparency rules?
Source · eur-lex.europa.eu

2 December 2027

EU AI Act: high-risk recruitment and employment systems

Employer impact
High-risk AI obligations are due to apply to covered recruitment and employment systems, including certain tools used for hiring, promotion and worker management. Providers and deployers will need to meet their respective requirements.
Service relevance
Recruitment process outsourcing, hiring advisory, assessment services, AI governance consulting and recruiter training.
Content opportunity
What should employers ask recruitment partners about AI-assisted hiring decisions?
Source · digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu

Following the 2 December 2026 transposition deadline, national rules on platform-worker employment status and algorithmic management will affect digital labour platforms during 2027. Requirements will vary by member state.

Contingent labour consulting, platform-work advisory, contractor engagement and workforce management services.
Content opportunity
